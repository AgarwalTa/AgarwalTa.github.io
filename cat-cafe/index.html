03 / welfare lab

Design around
choice.

Five practical pillars frame the simulation. Click a pillar to reveal why it matters; then test the idea inside the Room Lab.

🛋️

Retreat

Spaces where a resident can disengage.

A retreat is useful only if it can actually be used without being blocked, chased or turned into a display.
🪜

Verticality

Perches and routes create spatial options.

Height can provide movement and observation choices. The simulation rewards accessible routes rather than one mandatory perch.
🍽️

Resources

Food, water, rest and toileting are separated.

The game treats resource placement as a design problem: availability and separation matter more than decoration.
🧩

Enrichment

Novelty, play and foraging choices.

Rotate activities rather than forcing interaction. Different residents have different play drives and novelty tolerance.
🌿

Sensory calm

Sound, smell, touch and cleaning.

The simulation tracks stimulation as a contextual signal. A quiet room can be the right answer for one resident and a boring one for another.
